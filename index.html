TB Resilience Dashboard

A decision-support dashboard for tuberculosis across Aceh and its 23 districts. It pairs an SEIR epidemic model with a stigma model to project case load a year ahead, accounts for disaster status, and lets planners test a psychosocial intervention before committing to it. Assumptions are labelled as assumptions, and districts with no data show as missing rather than as zero.
